SU budgets: for helpdesk staff and ColdFront admins

Who you are + scope

You answer the questions a budget raises: "why is my job pending", "the Portal says I still have SU but sbalance says I am over", "why was I charged more than my cap". You may also be the person who changes a budget: a ColdFront staff member or superuser who approves allocations, raises Extra Budget (SU), sets or clears a spend cap, or asks a cluster admin to reset usage. The PI's own view of the cap is in pi.md §4; the user's view of sbalance is in user.md §4. This guide is the layer underneath both: where each number comes from, what Slurm actually enforces, and what you can change.

Everything here describes the pooled SU model: one budget per allocation, in service units (SU), where 1 SU = 1 billing-hour = $0.001 and a job's billing is max(cpus x W_cpu, mem x W_mem, gpus x W_gpu) from the partition's TRESBillingWeights. The per-cluster values this guide refers to (reset period, decay, whether safe is set) come from scontrol show config, never from memory.

Quick start

1. Know the three numbers before you answer

The same account in the same month has three correct figures. Say which one you mean.

Figure What it is How to read it Who shows it
Slurm counter What slurmctld has counted against the budget. Debited every PriorityCalcPeriod while a job runs; decays with PriorityDecayHalfLife; zeroed by PriorityUsageResetPeriod. This is the number that decides whether a new job is accepted. scontrol show assoc_mgr accounts=<acct> flags=assoc → account row, GrpTRESMins=...billing=LIMIT(USED); GrpTRESRunMins=...billing=N(RESERVED) is what running jobs still hold Portal bar and "available" on Allocation Detail, interact, cli_filter at submit, sbalance --detail ("Slurm count"), the sbatch gate itself
Accounting total TRES-minutes the slurmdbd rollup credited to the account in the period, sub-accounts included. No decay, clipped to the window, includes every run of a requeued job. slurmdbd adds each hour at the top of the next one, so it trails real time by up to an hour. The figure a bill is based on. sreport -T billing cluster AccountUtilizationByUser account=<acct> start=<period start> end=now -P (row with an empty Login = the account) sbalance "Used", Portal "N SU used this period", Budget (SU) usage on Project Detail and Submit Job (via the 15-min sync)
Per-job sum AllocTRES billing x elapsed / 3600 per job record. sacct -X -D -A <acct> -S <period start> --format=JobID,Elapsed,AllocTRES -P (-X drops steps, -D keeps the earlier runs of requeued jobs; without -D the sum is low) Job Metrics, the invoice (SlurmJob.billing_su), ad-hoc scripts

Rule of thumb: Used = accounting total, Available = limit minus the Slurm counter minus the SU held by running jobs. Both appear together on purpose; neither is wrong.

2. Explain why Available is not Limit minus Used

With a non-zero PriorityDecayHalfLife, the Slurm counter gives older usage less weight (weight = 2^(-age / half-life)), while the accounting total does not. So an account can show 182,000 SU used against a 180,000 budget and still have 36,000 SU available: the usage from early in the period has already lost part of its weight. The tolerance depends on when the usage happened. With a 30-day half-life over a 30-day period:

Usage pattern in the period Weight left at period end Accounting total when Slurm starts holding jobs
All in the last few days ~0.95 ~1.0 x limit
Spread evenly 0.72 ~1.39 x limit
Account kept at the limit from day 1 the decay frees ~2.3% of the limit per day ~1.69 x limit

These are derived from the documented half-life; Slurm's manual only states that half the usage is cleared at that age. Read the live values:

scontrol show config | grep -E 'PriorityDecayHalfLife|PriorityUsageResetPeriod|PriorityCalcPeriod|AccountingStorageEnforce'

With PriorityDecayHalfLife=0 the two figures coincide and a budget is exact; the trade-off (fair-share, utilisation, user experience) is a site decision, not something to change for one ticket.

Usually the partition's AllowAccounts (org-split GPU partitions), a missing user association, an account not created yet (allocation not approved, or a paid allocation whose cap is still 0), or a job_submit.lua rejection scontrol show partition <p> (the Allow* lines); sacctmgr show assoc where user=<u> account=<acct>; slurmctld log for the lua message Use a partition the account may reach; add the user to the allocation; approve the allocation or have the PI set a cap / No cap; read the lua reason

Job killed at the budget AccountingStorageEnforce lacks safe: when the limit is reached running jobs are killed instead of new ones being held scontrol show config (AccountingStorageEnforce) Cluster admin adds safe; until then warn PIs near their limit
Pending, Priority / Resources / ReqNodeNotAvail Normal queueing, not a budget hold squeue -p <partition> --start Nothing to do on the budget side

At submit. On the login node (sbatch, srun, salloc, interact), cli_filter prints a [BUDGET] note, in SU only, when the job needs more SU than the account has left: limit minus Slurm counter minus held. The estimate is the partition's TRESBillingWeights (largest of CPU, memory and GPU, the MAX_TRES rule; allocated CPUs when the partition has no weights) times the time limit, times the task count of an --array. It appears for free and paid jobs alike, never refuses and never prompts; under safe the job then waits on AssocGrpBillingMinutes. CLI_FILTER_SHOW_BALANCE=0 turns it off, and so does the filter's enforce_billing switch, with everything else. Portal Submit Job, OnDemand and REST submits do not run the filter.

4. Give a lab more room this period

Everything the Portal writes reaches Slurm through the sync (every 15 min, and immediately on an allocation change). Check the result with sbalance -p <pi> or scontrol show assoc_mgr accounts=<acct> flags=assoc.

Lever Who Where Effect in Slurm
Spend cap (Weekly Cap ($) / Weekly Cap (SU)) PI or staff Allocation Detail → Set Cap / Update Cap / No cap GrpTRESMins=billing = min(budget + extra, cap) x 60. 0 = not set (submission held), -1 = no cap
Extra Budget (SU) staff Allocation Detail → Allocation Attributes Added to the budget for this period only in the sense that the counter resets with the period; the attribute itself stays until you clear it
Budget (SU) staff Allocation Attributes The granted budget. An explicit 0 means unlimited on any tier (the cap is then the only ceiling); only an allocation with no Budget (SU) attribute at all falls back to the cluster seed
Reset the counter cluster admin, by agreement sacctmgr modify account where name=<acct> set RawUsage=0 Zeroes the Slurm counter for the account (budget and fair-share history). Never for one job; only when a budget was set wrong and the PI should not pay for it
Hold / release submission ColdFront only automatic (cap unset, IO paused) GrpSubmitJobs=0 / -1. Do not set it by hand: the sync owns that zero and will undo a manual value

Do not edit GrpTRESMins with sacctmgr by hand: the next sync rewrites it from the attributes. Do not tick is_billable to make something free: use a no-charge IO or an IO pause (see pi.md).

5. Answer the PI (ready to paste)

"The Portal says 36,000 SU available but sbalance says I used 182,000." Both are right. "Used" is the accounting total for the period (what a bill would be based on). "Available" is what Slurm will still accept: Slurm gives older usage less weight as the period goes on, so it can still admit work after the accounting total has passed the budget. The budget resets on <reset date>.

"My jobs have been pending since yesterday with AssocGrpBillingMinutes." Your allocation reached its SU limit for this period (the smaller of the budget and your spend cap). Jobs start again on <reset date>, when the counter clears, or sooner if you raise the cap on the allocation page (Update Cap) or ask us for extra budget. Running jobs are not affected.

"My job was killed with 'group max TRES(billing) minutes'." The allocation reached its SU limit while the job was running and the cluster is configured to stop running work at the limit. We are sorry about the lost run. To avoid it, set a cap with headroom or ask for extra budget before a large campaign; sbalance -p <pi> --detail shows how much is left and how much running jobs still hold.

6. Check the billing side

  • The invoice sums SlurmJob.billing_su per allocation and month (Compute Billing on the Billing Report; NODE_FAIL / BOOT_FAIL excluded). It follows the accounting total, not the Slurm counter, so with decay an invoice can exceed the cap the PI set. Whether invoices are capped at min(usage, cap) is a billing policy; check the Billing Report before promising either.
  • SlurmJob keeps one row per job. If a preemptible (scavenger) job was requeued, only its last run is in that row while sreport counts every run. Compare sacct -X -D with sreport -T billing for the month before trusting a per-job sum in a dispute.
  • The spend cap card reads the accounting total from slurmdbd's usage rollup (through slurmrestd, the same tables sreport reads) and reuses it for 15 minutes per web worker. It never reads job records, and nothing is stored on the allocation, so it is not a ledger.
  • On a project account that also has subproject accounts, Used and Available include the subprojects: the cap on that account applies to them too. Invoices stay per account.

Weekly cap with a period ceiling

Slurm checks the spend cap against the one counter that clears on the cluster's PriorityUsageResetPeriod, so without proration the cap covers that whole period. With Admin > Slurm Clusters > Weekly cap prorated on, the attribute is a weekly amount and the sync writes cap x days in the period / 7 as the limit (Slurm enforces that; the weekly figure is advisory). Turn it on by ticking the box (superuser; the field help says how many caps change) or with coldfront prorate_weekly_caps --cluster <name> (dry run) then --yes. Either way every positive stored cap becomes cap x 7 / days in the same transaction, so the limit in force does not move; unticking or --undo --yes reverses it.
